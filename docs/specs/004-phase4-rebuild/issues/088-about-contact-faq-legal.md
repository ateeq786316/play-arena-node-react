# 088 — About + Contact + FAQ + Legal

**Type:** AFK | **Blocked by:** 002

## What to build

Build static pages for the public website:

**About** (`/about`): Company mission, story (how PlayArena started), team section (placeholder), stats (grounds, users, bookings — hardcoded for now), values.

**Contact** (`/contact`): Contact form (name, email, subject, message) → sends email to support. WhatsApp link (floating button). Email address, physical address (placeholder).

**FAQ** (`/faq`): Accordion-style FAQ grouped by category (For Players, For Owners, Payments, Account, Technical). Each question expands to show answer.

**Terms of Service** (`/terms`): Legal terms content (placeholder, standard SaaS terms).

**Privacy Policy** (`/privacy`): Privacy content (placeholder, standard).

All pages use the design system components. Responsive. Footer links to all pages.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] About page with story and stats
- [ ] Contact form submits to support email
- [ ] FAQ page with accordion
- [ ] Terms and Privacy pages
- [ ] All pages responsive and using design system
- [ ] Footer links to all pages
